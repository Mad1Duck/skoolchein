Scholarship Protocol is a decentralized funding platform that lets communities collectively decide who gets educational support — and ensures the money is only released when progress is actually made.

Traditional scholarship systems are broken in three ways. First, opacity. Donors send money to institutions and never see where it goes. Second, no accountability. Recipients often receive a lump sum upfront with no obligation to deliver. Third, gatekeeping. A small committee decides who is worthy, excluding voices from the actual community.

This protocol fixes all three. Donors contribute directly to a scholarship pool and gain voting rights proportional to their contribution. They vote on which applicants deserve funding. The more you donate, the more influence you have. But influence is transparent — every vote is recorded on-chain.

Applicants do not ask for a handout. They propose a plan. Each applicant breaks their funding needs into milestones — for example, research phase, tuition payment, equipment purchase, final deliverable. Each milestone has a specific amount and a proof requirement. Funds are locked until the milestone is completed. Recipients withdraw incrementally as they deliver, not all at once.

When a dispute arises over whether a milestone was truly completed, a decentralized arbitration system resolves it. Three independent arbiters are randomly selected. They vote using a commit-reveal scheme so no one can see others' votes before casting their own. This prevents collusion between arbiters and applicants.

Donors are not just charitable — they are engaged stakeholders. Every contribution earns a commemorative digital collectible that recognizes their support level. These collectibles serve as public proof of participation and can unlock community privileges.

The platform includes a discovery interface where anyone can browse active scholarship programs, view applicant profiles and their milestone plans, cast votes, and track fund distribution in real time. Program creators can launch new scholarship rounds with custom criteria. Applicants can apply with structured milestone proposals. Donors can contribute and vote. Disputes can be raised and resolved fairly.

Scholarship Protocol turns charitable giving into a transparent, accountable, community-governed system. No black boxes. No lump-sum handouts. No closed-door committees. Just donors, applicants, and code — with the community in charge.
